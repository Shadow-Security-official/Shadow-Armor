    How the grade is computed

    weight(severity) critical 10 · high 5 · medium 3 · low 1 · info 0 evaluated pass, fail and error controls (not-applicable and waived are left out; an error counts as a failure) score 100 × Σ weight(pass) / Σ weight(evaluated) raw grade A ≥ 90 · B ≥ 80 · C ≥ 65 · D ≥ 50 · E below caps a critical control failing → at most D an A that rests on runtime-only passes → B (it rests on them when it would not stay A if every runtime-only pass failed after a reboot) grade the worse of the raw grade and every cap Qualified verdicts say what a PASS proves, in three columns: running now, on disk, after a reboot. PASS·durable compliant now, and the persisted state keeps it after a reboot (or a real reboot proved it) PASS·runtime-only compliant now, but the persisted state contradicts it or nothing on disk proves it FAIL·pending wrong now, but the persisted state is already compliant: a reboot or reload is pending

    The CLI computes the same numbers with the same formula (internal/score/score.go and this page's score.js are tested for parity).
